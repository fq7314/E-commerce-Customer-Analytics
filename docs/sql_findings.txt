We started with a Online Retail 2 CSV from kaggle also found in data

I started cleaning the data and found:
- The cleaned dataset contains 805,549 valid purchase rows.
- The cleaning process removed missing customer IDs, cancelled invoices, returns, and invalid price or quantity rows.
- A revenue column was created by multiplying Quantity by Price.

Revenue Analysis
-- Total revenue : $17743429.18

- Monthly revenue seemed to show pattern with the highest revenue in November 2010 at 1,172,336.04.
- The results showed that Sep to Nov seemed to be high periods, possibly suggesting holiday shopping


- The top revenue product was REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER, generating 286,486.30 in revenue.
- WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER was also a major driver, with 252,072.46 in revenue and 93,640 units sold.
- PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE appears to be a large one-time order because it generated 168,469.60 in revenue from only one order.
- Some high-revenue rows, such as Manual and POSTAGE, may represent service or charges rather than normal products.

- There were 1,623 one-time buyers, representing 27.61% of all customers.
- One-time buyers spent an average of 350.08, which gives a baseline for comparing them against repeat buyers.

- Repeat buyers generated about 96.8% of total revenue, while one-time buyers generated only about 3.2%.
- AVG revenue of one-time: 350.08 , AVG revenue of repeat: 4,036.49
- This suggests that repeat buyers are the main revenue driver. 
- This helps us see that allowing one-time buyers to become a repeat buyer heavily increases our sales
- Both one timers and repeaters buy the product REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER the most
